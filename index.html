How to type units
  • Powers: cm^3, cm3 or cm³. Rates: mi/h, m/s^2, g/mL.
  • Name the substance after the unit: g NaCl, mol H2O, molecules CO2, L solution.
  • Big and small numbers: 6.022e23 or 6.022x10^23.
  • To raise a whole factor to a power (for squared or cubed units), use the small ^1 box in its corner. Leave it at 1 unless the units call for more.
